AINEO v43.2.34 — make_tracks Share Generation Audit

Root problem:
- tracks.json was updated by make_tracks.py, but /share/song, /share/cards/song, and /share/cards/story were not regenerated by the tool.
- New songs therefore appeared in the app/library, but did not get song share pages or share cards.

Repair:
- make_tracks.py now imports the share generator directly.
- Every successful make_tracks.py run generates a share HTML page for every track in tracks.json.
- Every successful make_tracks.py run generates the wide song share SVG and vertical story SVG for every track in tracks.json.
- The build report now records share_generation counts.

Confirmed generated for newly added songs:
- share/song/intertwined.html
- share/song/is-it-faith.html
- share/song/promise-in-the-sky.html
- share/cards/song/intertwined.svg
- share/cards/song/is-it-faith.svg
- share/cards/song/promise-in-the-sky.svg
- share/cards/story/intertwined.svg
- share/cards/story/is-it-faith.svg
- share/cards/story/promise-in-the-sky.svg

Validation:
- tracks.json count: 75
- share/song pages: 75
- share/cards/song SVGs: 75
- share/cards/story SVGs: 75
- Missing share assets for current tracks: 0

Usage:
1. Add audio to /audio.
2. Add/adjust optional entries in track-metadata.json.
3. Run: python make_tracks.py --report-json
4. Deploy the regenerated app files, including /share.
